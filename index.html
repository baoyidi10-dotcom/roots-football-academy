学院理念Our Philosophy

从街头足球到现代科学From street football to modern science

我们的根Our Roots

在葡萄牙和巴西，足球从街头开始，用纸做的球、用书包摆的球门。正是这种纯粹的热爱，孕育了世界上最具创造力的球员。我们把这种文化带到广州，让孩子先爱上足球，再成为球员。 In Portugal and Brazil, football starts in the street: paper balls, schoolbags for goalposts. That pure passion produced the world's most creative players. We bring this culture to Guangzhou. Children fall in love with the game first, then become players.

我们的方法Our Method

热爱之上是专业：我们将现代运动科学、系统的技术基本功训练与欧洲青训体系相结合，为每个年龄段设计科学的训练计划，并定期向家长反馈孩子的成长。 On top of passion comes professionalism. We combine modern sports science, systematic technical fundamentals and European youth-development methodology, with age-appropriate training plans and regular progress feedback to parents.

家长常见问题For Parents

家长最常问的问题Questions parents ask

我的孩子适合哪个班？Which group is right for my child?
我们按年龄与水平分组。请通过微信联系我们，说明孩子的年龄与足球经验，我们会为您推荐合适的班级。没有任何足球基础也完全没问题，启蒙体系正是为此设计的。Groups are organised by age and level. Message us on WeChat with your child's age and football experience and we will advise the right group. No previous experience is needed, the Discovery pathway is built exactly for that.
训练用什么语言？What language are sessions taught in?
训练以英语和中文进行。我们的教练可用英语、葡萄牙语、法语和中文直接沟通，孩子在运动中自然接触英语环境。Sessions run in English and Mandarin. Our coaches speak English, Portuguese, French and Mandarin directly, so children pick up English naturally through sport.
在哪里训练？Where do you train?
主训练场地为 Arena Roots，位于广州。具体地址与交通方式请通过微信咨询。Our home venue is Arena Roots in Guangzhou. Message us on WeChat for the exact address and directions.
课程包包含什么？What does a course package include?
小组班课程包为10节课，每节课由葡萄牙或巴西教练亲自带训，并包含定期的家长反馈与成长记录。有效期与退费规则请咨询顾问。A group package is 10 sessions, each led directly by our Portuguese or Brazilian coaches, with regular parent feedback and progress tracking. Ask our team about validity and refund policy.
孩子可以参加国际赛事吗？Can my child travel to international tournaments?
可以。2026年6月我们已带队赴西班牙参加 MICFootball7 与 FOOTVIA 国际锦标赛。签证信、监护文件与保险均由学院内部处理。名额有限，需提前规划。Yes. In June 2026 we took a squad to Spain for MICFootball7 and the FOOTVIA International Tournament. Visa letters, guardianship documents and insurance are handled in-house. Places are limited and require advance planning.
我们学校想开设足球课程，如何合作？Our school wants a football program. How do we start?
我们与学校合作开设课后班与 ECA / CCA 课程。学院为正式注册的法律主体，可签署协议并开具发票。请发送邮件至 rootsfootballacademy@proton.me，我们48小时内回复。We run after-school and ECA / CCA programs with schools. Roots is a registered legal entity able to sign agreements and issue invoices (发票). Email rootsfootballacademy@proton.me and we will reply within 48 hours.
