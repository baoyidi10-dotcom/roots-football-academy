学院理念Our Philosophy

从街头足球到现代科学From Street Football to Modern Science

🌍 我们的根🌍 Our Roots

在葡萄牙和巴西，足球从街头开始--用纸做的球、用书包摆的球门。正是这种纯粹的热爱，孕育了世界上最具创造力的球员。我们把这种文化带到广州，让孩子先爱上足球，再成为球员。 In Portugal and Brazil, football starts in the street - paper balls, schoolbag goalposts. That pure passion produced the world's most creative players. We bring this culture to Guangzhou: children fall in love with the game first, then become players.

🔬 我们的方法🔬 Our Method

热爱之上是专业：我们将现代运动科学、系统的技术基本功训练与欧洲青训体系相结合，为每个年龄段设计科学的训练计划，并定期向家长反馈孩子的成长。 On top of passion comes professionalism: we combine modern sports science, systematic technical fundamentals and European youth-development methodology, with age-appropriate training plans and regular progress feedback to parents.

ID

Idris 教练（葡萄牙）Coach Idris (Portugal)

联合创始人 · 主教练Co-Founder · Head Coach

葡萄牙职业青训教练，曾在法国、韩国执教，2015年起扎根中国。中文流利，深刻理解中国家庭的教育期望，是孩子与家长都信任的沟通桥梁。 Portuguese youth coach with professional experience in France and South Korea, based in China since 2015. Fluent in Mandarin, he bridges European football culture and the expectations of Chinese families.

RP

Rodrigo Paixão Mesquita 教练（巴西）Coach Rodrigo Paixão Mesquita (Brazil)

联合创始人 · 技术总监Co-Founder · Technical Director

出生于巴西圣保罗的职业前腰球员，从国际体育俱乐部（Internacional）青训体系出道，随后代表国际队与菲格伦斯竞技（Figueirense）征战巴西甲级联赛，并三次夺得南里奥格兰德州联赛冠军（2003、2004、2005年）。自2009年起，他在中国职业联赛效力长达九年，先后效力沈阳东进、安徽九方、哈尔滨毅腾、贵州智诚与浙江毅腾等俱乐部，随后转型出任中国职业乙级俱乐部助理教练。他将地道的巴西技术流打法与职业标准，带进根源足球学院的每一堂训练课。 Born in São Paulo, Brazil, Rodrigo came through Sport Club Internacional's youth academy and went on to play in Brazil's top flight for Internacional and Figueirense, winning the Campeonato Gaúcho three times (2003, 2004, 2005). From 2009 he built a nine-year career in Chinese professional football with clubs including Shenyang Dongjin, Anhui Jiufang, Harbin Yiteng, Guizhou Zhicheng and Zhejiang Yiteng, before transitioning into coaching as assistant coach of a Chinese professional second-division club. He brings authentic Brazilian technical methodology and professional standards to every Roots session.
